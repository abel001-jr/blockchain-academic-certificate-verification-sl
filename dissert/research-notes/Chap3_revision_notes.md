A publication-record search checks the 1996 book citation. No benchmark of 68, invented achieved score or unsupported reliability coefficient is reported.

The revision does not fabricate new research participants, research dates or test outcomes. It adds research grounding for the methodology and a clearer evaluation design, not newly collected empirical data.

These values remain historical draft claims pending the records identified in E1–E8 of the combined workflow.
